COMPLIMENTARY EDUCATIONAL REFERENCE ($0)

US Brokerage Limit-Order & ETF Terminology Guide

I. Limit Orders vs. Market Orders
On self-directed platforms such as Fidelity, Charles Schwab, or Vanguard, a Limit Order specifies a maximum purchase price rather than accepting temporary bid-ask spread widening during the first 15 minutes after the 9:30 AM ET open.
II. Non-Leveraged 1x Spot Sector ETFs
Both SMH (Semiconductors) and XLV (US Healthcare) are unleveraged 1x spot ETFs free from daily 3x leveraged volatility-reset decay.
III. Short-Term US Treasury Liquidity Reference
In historical case studies, short-term household reserve buckets are commonly modeled using 0–3 month US Treasury bills (SGOV) or government money-market funds (SPAXX / SWVXX / VMFXX).
